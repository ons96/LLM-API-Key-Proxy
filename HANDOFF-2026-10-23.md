### Fix made

`ProviderStatusTracker` previously kept incrementing failure counts even after a
successful provider check. Once a provider reached the failure threshold, it
could remain reported as `down` forever. The fix resets consecutive failures on
success and only applies the `down` threshold to failed checks.
